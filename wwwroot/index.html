Network economy

A social network that pays the people who run it.

Big platforms keep the ad money and sell your attention. Deltanium has no ads: people pay small fees in D for storage, bandwidth and transactions, and most of every fee goes straight to whoever provides that service.

80%

Run a Store node

Keep encrypted copies of posts and files. You earn 80% of storage fees (800 D per GB per month, per copy) and 85% of bandwidth fees (300 D per GB served), released monthly from escrow.

Fees

Run a Block Creator

Validate and seal transactions into blocks. Transaction fees collect in the block fee pool and are paid to the Block Creator that seals the block.

90%

Create content

Set a monthly subscription or receive tips. You keep 90% — no ad algorithm decides what you are worth.

800 D

Pay for what you store

Storage costs 800 D (800 VND) per GB per month per copy, paid from your wallet. Every item you store (post, reaction, comment, message) costs at least 1 D, and the app shows the exact amount before it is charged.

D: a stable unit, not a lottery ticket

  • 1 D = 1 VND Every D in circulation is backed 1:1 by VND in reserve. The reserve and circulating supply are public (api.deltanium.com/api/wallet/reserve).
  • Earnings you can cash out Verified node operators and creators can redeem D to VND (1% fee, from 100,000 D).
  • Books anyone can check The ledger is anchored into blocks, so balances can be proven against the chain.

What you need to run a node

  • A machine that stays on 24/7 — a VPS or a computer at home.
  • A public address with HTTPS (a domain name pointing to that machine).
  • .NET 8 and MySQL installed.
  • Free disk space: as much as you want to offer.
  • A free voucher: a one-time registration code from the public list.

Up and running in 4 steps

  1. Pick an unused voucher from the public list.
  2. Download the node code from GitHub.
  3. Set your address and voucher, then start the node — it registers itself.
  4. Find your node on the live Nodes page.

Peace of mind

We cannot read what you lock.

Think of it like sealing a letter before you send it. Your device locks the content; only the people you allow can open it. The network moves sealed packages — it does not hold the letter opener.

  • No silent snooping Private posts and chats are not open to staff, advertisers, or “recommendation engines”.
  • Your account travels with you You keep a recovery phrase. Lose a phone, keep your identity — without begging a support team.
  • Built for people first The tech is under the hood. You just post, follow, and talk — with stronger defaults.

Simple idea

You → lock → share → friends unlock

Everyone else on the path only sees a sealed package. That is the whole privacy story.

Testnet

Try everything on the testnet first

A separate network with its own accounts, test coins and nodes. Signatures are bound to the network, so nothing done on testnet can be replayed on mainnet. Testnet data may be reset at any time.
