Peace of mind

We cannot read what you lock.

Think of it like sealing a letter before you send it. Your device locks the content; only the people you allow can open it. The network moves sealed packages — it does not hold the letter opener.

  • No silent snooping Private posts and chats are not open to staff, advertisers, or “recommendation engines”.
  • Your account travels with you You keep a recovery phrase. Lose a phone, keep your identity — without begging a support team.
  • Built for people first The tech is under the hood. You just post, follow, and talk — with stronger defaults.

Simple idea

You → lock → share → friends unlock

Everyone else on the path only sees a sealed package. That is the whole privacy story.
