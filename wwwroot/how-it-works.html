How it works

A social network where the locks are on your side.

No jargon needed. Four kinds of machines work together, and only your device holds the key to your private content.

The big picture

Four parts, one network

🔑

You & your app

Your phone or browser. It holds your key, locks what you post and unlocks what is shared with you.

Holds your private key
📇

Directory (Central)

Like a phone book: who is who, who follows whom, and where each sealed copy is kept. It cannot open your private posts.

🗄️

Store nodes

Computers that keep sealed copies of posts, photos and offline messages — at least two copies on different machines.

Paid from storage fees
📒

Block Creators

Keep a shared ledger of actions and payments that anyone can check, so no one can quietly rewrite history.

Paid from transaction fees

Anyone can run a Store node or a Block Creator and earn from it — see the live list on the Nodes page.

Step by step

What happens when you post

  1. 1

    You write

    A post, a photo, a message — on your own device.

  2. 2

    Your device locks it

    It is encrypted before it leaves your phone. What travels is a sealed package.

  3. 3

    Copies are stored

    The sealed package goes to two Store nodes, so one going offline loses nothing.

  4. 4

    The Directory notes where

    It records which Stores hold the package — not what is inside.

  5. 5

    Friends open it

    The people you chose fetch the package and unlock it on their own devices.

Money

Where the money goes

1. Top up

Bank transfer (VietQR) or PayPal. 100,000 VND becomes 97,000 D (3% fee).

2. D in your wallet

1 D = 1 VND, backed 1:1 by money held in reserve. Storage: 200 D per GB per month per copy, billed monthly.

3. Pay for what you use

Extra storage, bandwidth, tips and subscriptions. Every fee is split:
